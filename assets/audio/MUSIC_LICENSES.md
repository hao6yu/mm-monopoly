# Music licenses

## City theme collection

The following tracks were composed by Juhani Junkala and released under the
[Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- `music/city_home_town.mp3` — “Home Town”
- `music/city_timeless.mp3` — “Where Time Stands Still”
- `music/city_sunshine_coast.mp3` — “Sunshine Coast”
- `music/city_bazaar.mp3` — “Bazaar”

Source: [JRPG Pack 2 — Towns on OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/jrpg-pack-2-towns)

The source OGG files were converted to 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps MP3 and normalized to
approximately -18 LUFS for consistent in-game playback. Attribution is not
required by CC0, but the composer and license are retained here and in each
file's metadata for provenance.

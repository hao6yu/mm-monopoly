Saving and continuing

Use the in-game menu to save your progress, then choose Continue from the main menu. Saves stay on your device. Do not uninstall the app when troubleshooting if you want to keep your game data.

Moving around the 3D board

Drag with one finger to move the camera, use two fingers to rotate, and pinch to zoom. Use the view controls to return to the player. Camera following and graphics quality can be adjusted in Settings.

Sound and optional permissions

Music and sound effects can be controlled in the game. Camera/photo access is optional for custom avatars. Local notifications are optional and can be disabled in your device settings.

Offline play and privacy

No account is required to play. Gameplay works offline and has no ads. Game saves and custom avatars stay on the device; the operating system may include them in your own device backup.

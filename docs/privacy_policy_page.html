Privacy Policy — M&M Property Tycoon

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

M&M Property Tycoon is an offline board game. It runs entirely on your device and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your game saves, settings, statistics, leaderboard entries, and custom avatars are stored only on this device. They are never sent to the developer or to any server, and they are removed when you delete the app. If you back up your device (for example iCloud or a Google backup), your operating system may include this app data in your own backup.

Permissions

The camera and photo library are used only if you choose to create a custom avatar photo; photos stay on your device. Notifications are optional local reminders you can turn off in your device settings. The game does not read your contacts, location, microphone, or any other personal data.

No data collection

The app does not collect, share, or sell personal data, and does not contact any server. Opening the optional support link leaves the app and is subject to that website's own policies.

Children

The game is family-friendly and contains no chat, user accounts, or external content beyond the optional support link.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach the developer through the support link in the app's Settings screen or the contact options in the app store listing.

This page is the store-listing copy of the policy that is also available inside the app under Settings > Privacy Policy. It covers the same content in all app languages.
